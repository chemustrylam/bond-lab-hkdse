# Game Plan: Bond Lab - HKDSE Chemical Bonding & Electron Diagram Builder

## Overview
A web-based interactive chemistry learning game tailored for HKDSE Chemistry (Topic II: Microscopic World I - Chapter 7 Metallic & Ionic Bonding, Chapter 8 Covalent Bonding). Students practice drawing electron diagrams (both full-electron Bohr-Lewis diagrams and outermost-shell Lewis electron dot/cross diagrams), forming ions, building covalent molecules, and receiving instant auto-marked feedback based on HKDSE marking criteria.

## Features & Modes
1. **Interactive Electron Diagram Canvas**:
   - Add/remove atoms and select element (H to Ca, group 1-8 metals and non-metals).
   - Add/remove electron shells (rings) around atom cores.
   - Add electrons with type: Dot (•) or Cross (×).
   - Position electrons in standard 4-quadrant HKDSE pairing conventions (top, bottom, left, right).
   - Auto-pair mode / manual quadrant click mode.
   - Add Bond Pairs (shared electrons in overlapping shell regions for covalent bonds: single, double, triple bonds).
   - Add Lone Pairs (non-bonding valence pairs).
   - Enclose in square brackets `[ ... ]` and assign ionic charge (`+`, `2+`, `3+`, `-`, `2-`, `3-`).
   - Add stoichiometric coefficients (e.g., `2 [Cl]-` or multiple ions in MgCl2 / Na2O).
2. **HKDSE Exam Quest Mode (Guided Levels)**:
   - Level 1: Neutral Atoms & Electron Arrangement (e.g. Na 2,8,1, Cl 2,8,7, O 2,6).
   - Level 2: Formation of Cations (Na+, Mg2+, Al3+, K+, Ca2+) - outermost shell empty or noble gas shell with charge.
   - Level 3: Formation of Anions (Cl-, O2-, N3-, F-) - octet full, brackets & charge.
   - Level 4: Simple Ionic Compounds (NaCl, MgO, CaCl2, Na2O, Al2O3) - ratio of ions, charge balance, brackets.
   - Level 5: Covalent Molecules - Single Bonds (H2, Cl2, HCl, CH4, NH3, H2O).
   - Level 6: Covalent Molecules - Multiple Bonds (O2 double bond, N2 triple bond, CO2 double bonds).
   - Level 7: Challenging HKDSE Past Paper Style (NH4+, H3O+, PCl3, CCl4).
3. **Compound Builder & Auto-Checker**:
   - Real-time or on-demand automated grading matching HKDSE guidelines:
     * Correct number of shells / outermost shell only toggle.
     * Correct total electrons, dots and crosses differentiation.
     * Octet / duplet rule satisfied for all participating atoms.
     * Net charge neutrality for ionic compounds.
     * Proper brackets and sign placement.
     * Clear explanations of common student mistakes (e.g., "M1: missing brackets on ion", "M2: charge sign written as +2 instead of 2+", "M3: forgot to share 2 pairs for O=O double bond").
4. **Sandbox Lab (Free Mode)**:
   - Unrestricted laboratory where teachers can demonstrate any element/compound or challenge students in classroom projectors.
   - Export diagram as PNG or Copy Share Link.
   - Teacher Cheat Sheet & HKDSE Marking Tips modal.
